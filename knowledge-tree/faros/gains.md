GAINS™ (Generative AI Impact Net Score) is the named diagnostic referenced by the AI Transformation
capability: a three-step engagement (baseline + root cause → benchmark/ROI of interventions →
90-day action plan) scored across ten transformation dimensions, using interviews, surveys, and live
SDLC telemetry. Part service / part product — the "shared metric that aligns engineering and
finance" framing is the org-level expression of the repo's door/room positioning. Page cites the
"$40B invested, 5% see returns" paradox (marketing claim; echoes the MIT NANDA proof point already
in offering.json).
