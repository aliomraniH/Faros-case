**The shipped core.** Faros joins tickets → AI sessions → PRs → deploys → incidents → spend
into one graph, at session/file/decision grain. This is the pillar competitors can't match: a
dashboard sees activity, a survey sees sentiment, a catalog sees static context, a cost tool sees
the bill — none *join* the slices. Observe is also the foundation the other two pillars stand on
(Field Guide: observability is "the necessary first step to optimizing and governing").

Key sub-capabilities: **Token Attribution Ledger**, **Engineering World Model**, **Provenance/audit
trail**.
