The "door" capability for persona:maya — named on the live page as: "every token is tied to the
work it produced, the team that ran it, and the outcome it shipped." Already referenced by the
approved `mapping:maya-roi-ledger` but not yet its own node; this proposal gives that mapping a
first-class, page-sourced target. Classifies spend productive / inefficient / wasteful (each with a
dollar figure). Matches offering.json (shipped). Corroborates, does not conflict with, the seed.
