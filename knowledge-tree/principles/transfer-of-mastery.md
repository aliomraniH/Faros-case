The aspiration that anchors the close — 'your best teams haven't seen their best yet.' The
harness (context, memory, verification), not the model, determines reliability (Hashimoto). When an
agent makes a mistake, the job is to 'write a solution so it never happens again' — and the next
junior reads that rule and learns the reasoning, not just the patch.
