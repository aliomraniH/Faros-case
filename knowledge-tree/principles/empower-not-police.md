A culture rule the org wants protected and the advisor must honor: Faros is positioned as
*empower, not surveil*. Decisions are about repos, tools, and policy — never ranking people. The
junior reading a negative-memory rule and learning the reasoning is the emotional center of the
story for exactly this reason. (Tension to hold honestly: Faros does support org/team/**individual**
contextual views with RBAC — so the governance story matters more, not less.)
